Effective as of June 2, 2026 · Last updated: June 6, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Ande Games ("we", "us", "our", or the "Service Provider") collects, uses, and shares information when you use any of our mobile games or applications, including any related services we operate (collectively, the "Services" or the "Application"). This policy applies to all current and future games published under the Ande Games brand, including but not limited to Stick Sort.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. Our games do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, certain information is automatically collected to operate, monetize, and improve our games:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • The pages of the Application you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The total time spent in the Application
  • Your mobile operating system and version
  • Device model, system language, and country/region
  • Advertising identifier (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Application version

We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent before using non-essential tracking technologies. You can review or change your consent at any time from the in-game Settings menu (Privacy Options).
